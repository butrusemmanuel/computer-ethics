LEARN THE BASICS

Principles of computer ethics

Simple rules that guide responsible technology use.

🔐

Respect privacy

Never access another person’s private information without permission.

🛡️

Do no harm

Do not use computers or the internet to hurt others.

🤝

Be honest

Do not create or spread false information online.

©️

Respect ownership

Credit creators and never copy work without permission.

👤

Respect access rights

Only use accounts, files and systems you are allowed to use.

⚙️

Be responsible

Take responsibility for your actions when using technology.

STAY SAFE ONLINE

Computer privacy

Privacy means protecting personal information from people who are not authorized to see it. Personal information includes:

  • Names and addresses
  • Passwords
  • Phone numbers
  • Photographs
  • Financial information

WHY IT MATTERS

Benefits of computer ethics

Good digital habits make technology better for everyone.

  • Creates responsible computer users
  • Protects personal and organizational information
  • Helps reduce cybercrime and online abuse
  • Promotes respect and cooperation
  • Protects intellectual property
  • Improves online safety and confidence
